Short: Keep tasks alive (auto-revive)

New "Keep alive" option for a task: when its agent **process exits** — a crash, or a provider/proxy/network error that takes the process down — dev3 relaunches and resumes the conversation automatically, with exponential backoff, a wait for a known rate-limit reset before retrying (so it doesn't relaunch straight into a blocked account), and a retry cap that raises an attention badge when it finally gives up. On Windows it revives only a terminal that is gone altogether. It won't revive a still-running agent that is merely paused at a usage limit, and it never touches a hibernated task or one that is legitimately waiting on you. Toggle it from the task's right-click menu, filter the board with `is:keepalive`, or drive it from the CLI: `dev3 keep-alive on|off|status|list`.
