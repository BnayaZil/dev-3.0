Short: Go to tasks, not just projects

The ⇧⌘K "Go to" palette now has three modes — Projects, Tasks and Combined — on a Tab-switchable strip, each with its own shortcut (⇧⌘K projects, ⇧⌘J tasks, ⇧⌘L anything). Tasks are ordered most-recently-viewed first (persisted across app reloads) and searchable with the same token filters as the sidebar and board (label:, status:, agent:, is:, has:, type:, space:, priority: plus free text, #seq and PR number).
